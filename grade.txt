These are pretty good, but I think there's some misunderstanding about what these tests should represent. The way you call the methods and assertions in the test should demonstrate a behavior about your code. In many of these tests, the way you call the methods doesn't really prove anything to me about the way your code works. For example, in the test where you were to demonstrate that case doesn't matter, you entered the word "caT" and then later on asserted that the word "caT" was spelled correctly. This does not prove that the case was ignored, if anything it shows me that the case is not being ignored, since you're checking the exact same case. If "cat" is entered, then by asserting that "Cat" and "cAT" and "CaT" are considered properly spelled then that shows that the case doesn't matter.

Other tests also suffer from this, such as the ones that recommend words, and the ones that ignore whitespace.
